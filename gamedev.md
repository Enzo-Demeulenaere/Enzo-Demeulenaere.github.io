## Connect Four With a Twist !

As a challenge among friends, I recreated a Connect Four while adding a mystery twist to it: sometimes during a game, a mystery portal may appear above a random column, putting your token in this column will trigger one of 9 mystery powers that may completely change the course of a game, have fun discovering every one of them.
